## 🎖️ Contribution Areas We Recognize

We welcome and celebrate contributions across every layer of the civic tech stack:

- **Computer Vision & ML Pipeline**: YOLO11s architecture optimization, letterbox preprocessing, data augmentation, class stratification, and inference benchmarking.
- **Backend & Database Foundation**: Supabase schemas, PostGIS geospatial queries, server-authoritative Row Level Security (RLS) policies, and append-only audit ledgers.
- **Civic Logic & Fleet Dispatch**: Proximity calculations (Haversine formula), driver dispatch scheduling, arrival verification, and Safai Coins reward tokenomics.
- **API & Observability**: FastAPI endpoints, Pydantic v2 schemas, telemetry health probes, and edge runtime integration.
- **Testing & Quality Assurance**: Pytest test suites, pgTAP database tests, synthetic scene generators, and automated secret scanners.
- **Documentation & Community**: Architectural specifications, developer setup runbooks, Wiki guides, tutorials, and issue templates.

## 🤝 How to Join the Hall of Fame

1. Browse our curated [Good First Issues](https://github.com/TheVicky1/Safai_OS/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22) or pick an open issue from the tracker.
2. Follow the setup steps in our [Developer Guide](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) and review [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. Submit a pull request targeting `main`.
4. Once your pull request passes CI checks and is merged, you are permanently inducted into the **SafaiOS Contributors Hall of Fame**!
