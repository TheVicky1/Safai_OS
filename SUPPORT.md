# Support and Community Channels

Welcome to the **SafaiOS** support ecosystem. Whether you have architectural questions, need assistance with local environment configuration, or wish to explore a municipal deployment in your city, here is where to find support.

---

## Community and Discussion Channels

- **GitHub Discussions**: The primary channel for technical questions, design RFCs, model benchmarking, and general inquiries. Visit https://github.com/TheVicky1/Safai_OS/discussions.
- **GitHub Issues**: Reserved exclusively for confirmed bug reports, reproducible defects, and vetted feature proposals. Visit https://github.com/TheVicky1/Safai_OS/issues.
- **Wiki Knowledge Base**: Comprehensive documentation covering architecture, computer vision models, database schemas, and developer onboarding. Visit [SafaiOS Wiki](wiki/Home.md).
